Model

A competing-risks survival model, re-run at every visit: the chance of failure in each follow-up year, chained into risk at 2, 5 and 8 years.

Why this model

  • Survival, not a fixed-horizon classifier. It uses every observed year, even for patients followed for less than five.
  • Not a sequence model. Most patients have one or two echos, so a landmark model uses the history known at each visit without needing long sequences.
  • Primary: gradient boosting. Baseline: logistic regression.

How we chose the features

  • 1. Clinical input. Our clinician named the factors; we built all 42 the data can supply.
  • 2. Screening, built and rejected. Automatic selection was unstable across draws.
  • 3. Tested, then fixed. The clinician-aligned 21 won; scan frequency was dropped.
